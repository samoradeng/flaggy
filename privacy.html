Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Overview

Flagtriv is a flag guessing game. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about the limited data we collect.

Data We Collect

We collect anonymous usage data to improve the app:

  • Device identifier: A random ID generated on your device (not linked to you personally)
  • Game statistics: Scores, completion times, and game modes played
  • Platform: Whether you're using iOS, Android, or web

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Your name or email address (unless you optionally provide it for the leaderboard)
  • Your location
  • Your contacts
  • Any personal information

Leaderboard

If you choose to join the daily leaderboard, you provide a display name of your choice. This name is visible to other players on the leaderboard.

Data Storage

Your game progress is stored locally on your device. Anonymous analytics are stored securely on our servers (Supabase).

Third-Party Services

We use Supabase for our backend services. Their privacy policy can be found at supabase.com/privacy.

Children's Privacy

Flagtriv is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
