Frequently Asked Questions

When does the daily challenge reset?

The daily challenge resets at midnight UTC. Everyone around the world gets the same 5 flags each day.

How is my time calculated?

Your time is the total seconds spent actively playing. If you leave the app and come back, the timer pauses while you're away.

How do I share my results?

After completing the daily challenge, tap "Share Result" to copy your Wordle-style results to share with friends.

Can I play offline?

Yes! The daily challenge and practice mode work offline. You'll need internet to submit to the leaderboard or create multiplayer challenges.

How do multiplayer challenges work?

Create a challenge with your settings, share the code with friends, and see who can identify the flags fastest!

Is my data private?

Yes! We only collect anonymous analytics to improve the app. See our Privacy Policy for details.
